**Summary of Fog Computing:**

Fog computing enables localized processing and analysis of data, facilitating applications in sectors like healthcare, where forecasts suggest a market increase to $5.4 billion by 2017. It also supports wireless sensor networks needing actuator functionalities, offering a new paradigm that dramatically alters IT practices across services and data privacy.

Key areas of impact include:
1. Visualization and real-time analytics with global coverage supported by cloud technology.
2. Health care forecasting in the wake of growing demand for localized processing.
3. Integration of various technologies to meet the new requirements driven by device ubiquity and agile management.
4. Security discussions surrounding common attacks within fog computing environments.

This overview presents a comprehensive insight into how fog computing converges various technologies, heralding a shift in the IT landscape.
